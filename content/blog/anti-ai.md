Most of the people I respect fall somewhere on the spectrum between AI-skepticism, and anti-AI. I myself fall somewhere in this spectrum, but I've often found it hard to articulate where. I see mutuals saying that AI is *functionally* useful, but bad for society. Others staunchly claim that not only is it bad for society, it's also useless. Some even claim it will ALWAYS be useless, for all tasks, forever.

I find that even the quasi-accelerationists have bones to pick with AI. Usually functional ones (they don't seem to care too much about society), but still.

To clarify my thoughts -and to give you the opportunity to play along-, I've developed this small page, where you can drag around sliders, based on how compelling you find each anti-AI talking point. The sliders will default to my own placement, but you can drag the sliders around yourself, and then share the results if you wish.

The idea is that "good arguments" would constitute reasons to limit, restrict, ban, or otherwise regulate the AI industry, while "bad arguments" are essentially straw-men, and can be ignored.
